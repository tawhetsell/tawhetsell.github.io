Travis A. Whetsell

Assistant Professor, Jimmy and Rosalynn Carter School of Public Policy, Georgia Institute of Technology.

Open CV Selected work

Travis Whetsell's research focuses on public sector networks, science and technology policy, and the philosophy of public administration. His work has appeared in leading journals, including Journal of Public Administration Research and Theory, Public Administration Review, Perspectives on Public Management and Governance, Research Policy, and Science and Public Policy. He has conducted funded research supported by the National Science Foundation and the Georgia Department of Transportation.
